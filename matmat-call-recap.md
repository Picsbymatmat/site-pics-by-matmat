# What we're building

A **booking-focused site**, not another portfolio. Both your existing sites are portfolio-first, and what you actually need is somewhere people land and book you.

So the main purpose is booking; the work is on it as proof to show you're good  
at the kind of shoot someone's after. The full portfolio stays where it is, linked  
from the new site rather than rebuilt inside it.

Perhaps most people arriving will come from Instagram. That means the site will have to be amazing on mobile, and has to make sense to someone who already half-knows your work and just wants to book.

# Shoot categories

Four, replacing the older three:

1. Professional / product  
2. Stylized / lifestyle / brand  
3. Event  
4. Portraits

Each keeps its own visual feel, tied to the kind of work it is.

# What's on the site

- The four categories, each with a selection of your work  
- A booking flow: you pick the kind of shoot, then go from there  
- **Calendar** so people can see availability and book rather than waiting on a reply  
- **Testimonials** from past clients  
- **A results section** for the product/ecommerce work. Conversion numbers, before and

  after, that kind of thing

- **A link out to your existing portfolio**  
- Contact details, Instagram and socials
